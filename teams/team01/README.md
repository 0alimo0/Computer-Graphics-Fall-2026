  The east branch leads to the future planetarium.

## Planetarium site

The four flush corner markers on the east lawn outline a 28 × 32 m footprint.
On CU-1, its plot-local center is `(20, 0, -11)`, with `x` from 6 to 34 m
and `z` from -27 to 5 m. Face the entrance toward `+z` so it meets the paved
spur centered on `x = 20` at the front edge. The footprint has no colliders.

`PlotLayout` in `src/Plot.h` exposes the center and half-extents for placement.
Add the planetarium drawing alongside the observatory placement in `src/MyZone.cpp`.
